Chapter One · Palette

Maroon, gilt, and the
warmth of paper.

Six colors do the work. A deep oxblood anchors the brand; an aged gilt accents and signals; three paper-toned neutrals carry the long-form reading surfaces. Used sparingly, they read as old-world publishing rather than luxury cliché.

Chapter Two · Typography

A display face that
whispers, a body that listens.

Bodoni Moda for headlines — its high contrast and italics carry the editorial tone. Crimson Pro for running text — quietly modern, comfortable to read at length, and at home in italics. No sans serif. Ever.

Bodoni Moda · 300 / Italic 300 · Display

Finding
your perfect
home.

Used for hero statements, chapter openings, and moments where the brand needs to settle into the page. Tight tracking, generous leading, italics for emphasis.

Crimson Pro · 300 Italic · Body

"He listens before he leads — that is the whole craft."

Long form sets in 16–17px italic at 1.85 leading. Plain roman is reserved for legal text, captions, and metadata. Letter-spaced uppercase Crimson Pro becomes the small-caps voice of the brand.

Chapter Three · Mood

References for an
old-world quiet.

A loose composition of moments — gallery walls, golden hour through plantation shutters, a single brass fixture against limewash, the spine of a leather-bound book. Each tile holds a feeling more than an image.

"A home is not sold — it is found, and then quietly handed over."
— Editorial note, Vol. I

Chapter Four · References

The bookshelf
behind the brand.

A short list of the publications, properties, and pieces of design we keep returning to. None are imitated; all are inhaled.

01 Publication

Cabana Magazine

For its conviction that interiors deserve the same editorial weight as portraiture, and for its restraint with white space.

02 Type

Bodoni, after Massimo Vignelli

A reminder that one well-set display face will outlast a hundred trends. Italics earn their keep.

03 Place

River Oaks, after dusk

The way a single lit window reads against deep brick. The brand's whole color system, in one glance.

04 Object

A leather portfolio

Considered, slightly worn, gilt at the corners. Every page of the site should feel like turning one of its leaves.

05 Photography

Saul Leiter, late work

For warm shadow, partial obstruction, and the dignity of an unhurried frame.

06 Practice

A handwritten note

The closing gesture of every transaction. The site, at its best, is a printed version of the same instinct.
