⚖️ A quick note on Utah law

In Utah, a person may care for 4 or fewer children at a time without a state child care license or certificate. That's the limit we stay within, which is why we cap each session at 4 children (and no more than 2 little ones under age 3, per Utah's 2024 rules). Caring for 5–8 children would require a Residential Certificate and background checks, and 9+ would require a full Family Child Care license.

This information is provided to be transparent and is not legal advice. Rules can change — the official source is the Utah DHHS Division of Licensing & Background Checks (dlbc.utah.gov). We recommend confirming current requirements there before your visit.
